Privacy Policy for ScaleMate

Effective date: August 11, 2026

ScaleMate ("the App") is developed by Rocketsauce Studios ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

Summary

ScaleMate is a fully offline application. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, and the App does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account or sign-in is required to use the App.
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services are used.
  • No advertising SDKs are integrated into the App.
  • No data is transmitted over the internet by the App.

Your Data

All content you create in ScaleMate — including projects, dimensions, cut lists, and app settings — is stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party. If you export a CSV or PDF file, that file is created and saved locally on your device, under your control.

Uninstalling the App removes this locally stored data from your device, subject to your device's own backup or file-management behavior.

Children's Privacy

Since the App does not collect any personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If ScaleMate's data practices change in the future (for example, if a feature requiring data collection is added), this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly, and the effective date above will be revised.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: rocketsauce.gaming@gmail.com
